# Studio24: the local incumbent, and why a GLOWA subscription is worth paying

Researched 2026-09-24 from studio24.bg (home page, "for salons" and "salon
software" pages, a salon page), its Google Play listing, and public posts by
salons and customers. Figures are Studio24's own claims or third-party claims,
marked as such; none is verified. Nothing in GLOWA's public copy names
Studio24.

## What they are

- On the market since 2019. They claim 3,400+ Bulgarian salons, 8 million+
  bookings and 200,000+ visitors a month; the Android app shows 4.9 from about
  4,900 ratings. Bulgarian, English and Russian; Bulgaria only.
- **The salon software is free.** Calendar, online booking, CRM, stock,
  payroll, cash desk, reports, loyalty, vouchers, packages, waitlist, iframe,
  Google Reservations, email reminders - all at no charge. They charge for
  two things: new clients they bring (once, after the first visit) and SMS /
  Viber messages.
- The new-client fee is not published. Salons talk of "50% commission" (a
  Facebook group) and of paying €1,200 in commission in 30 days and then
  dropping to 272nd place when their paid promotion stopped (an Instagram
  reel). Unverified, but it says the ranking can be bought.
- Human onboarding: they build the salon's page, add the services, train the
  team and move data from other systems. Phone, chat and email support.
- A service × city landing page for hundreds of combinations - their main
  search traffic.
- A 2023 news item (rns.bg) reports a customer who missed a massage being
  emailed twice with a threat of blocking and phoned by staff asking why.

## What that means for GLOWA

GLOWA cannot be "cheaper" - it charges €6-24 a month against €0. It cannot
match seven years of catalogue, traffic and back-office features either. So
the subscription has to buy something "free" does not:

| Studio24's model | GLOWA's answer (all shipped) |
| --- | --- |
| Earns when a *new* client arrives; ranking can be paid for | Earns only the flat fee, so the product is built around *returning* clients: rebook invitations with real free times (§8q), waitlist offers (§8j). No paid ranking, no other salons on a salon's page, 0% commission. |
| No-shows handled by blocking and phoning the customer | Deposits by card at booking, refunded automatically on time, paid into the salon's own Stripe account (§8l). |
| The value of the paid part is hard to see | The dashboard's "what GLOWA brought you" counts online bookings, returns after an invitation, waitlist refills and retained deposits from the salon's own diary (§8q). |
| Pinch-zoom disabled (`user-scalable=no`), list-heavy pages | Zoom works; photo-first salon pages; "free today and tomorrow" on the landing page. |
| Bulgaria only | Bulgarian, English and Romanian, RON, Stripe for BG and RO. |

## Still missing against them (in priority order)

1. Viber / SMS reminders (a provider contract, then one channel adapter).
2. Service variants: short / medium / long hair with their own price and time.
3. Client import from a file, and "we move you for free" onboarding.
4. Service × city landing pages for search.
5. Ratings by category, several services in one visit, vouchers, Google
   Reservations. Stock, payroll and the cash desk are not worth chasing yet.
